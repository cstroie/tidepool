# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

First release.

- Ten rocks and a tide table: things settle and grow while the tide is in; five cairn stones
  decide which rocks are left alone when you wade out.
- The pool keeps the real time of the clock, so a week away is fourteen tides of change, counted
  when you come back (at most a capped number, in a few seconds of machine time).
- Seasons, a bell for a turning tide (B), naming rocks (N), the tide table (T), the journal (J),
  a new pool (R), and a walk-out when the program is left without Q.
- VT100 (line drawing, dim, bold, blink, reverse), VT52 and ADM-3A; without a clock device each
  visit is one tide.
- Messages kept inside the 80 columns, a centred intro.
